PulsePM Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to PulsePM ("we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our unified project management platform at pulsepm.in.

1. Information We Collect

When you use PulsePM, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, profile photo, and contact details provided during Google Sign-In or email registration.
  • Workspace & Project Data: Projects created, tasks assigned, subtasks, task statuses, team member lists, deadlines, and project chat messages.
  • Usage & Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and session timestamps to ensure optimal app performance and secure authentication.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the PulsePM project collaboration platform.
  • Authenticate user accounts securely via Google OAuth and email credentials.
  • Facilitate real-time team collaboration, task assignments, activity feeds, and chat notifications.
  • Persist user preferences such as Dark/Light theme mode.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

PulsePM does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Data is only shared with trusted cloud infrastructure providers (such as Google Cloud / Firebase) to deliver database storage and authentication services.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard SSL encryption and Firebase security rules to protect your personal information and project data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may update your profile information or delete your account at any time directly within the application settings.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support@pulsepm.in.
